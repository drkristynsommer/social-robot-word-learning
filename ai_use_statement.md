# Generative AI use statement

This note describes the use of generative artificial intelligence tools in the
preparation of the analysis code and manuscript associated with this deposit. It
is provided for transparency and corresponds to the declaration included in the
manuscript.

Generative AI assistance was used to support the organization of the analysis
pipeline, the drafting and editing of code and documentation, and the
preparation of supporting materials in this repository. All analytic decisions,
model specifications, interpretations, and substantive claims were made and
verified by the authors. Outputs generated with AI assistance were reviewed for
accuracy against the underlying data and statistical results.

No identifiable participant data were provided to any generative AI system at
any stage. Work involving individual-level data, including the de-identification
of datasets for release, was performed locally by the authors using the scripts
provided in this repository.

The tool used was Claude (Anthropic). As declared in the manuscript: "During
the preparation of this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic) to assist with
language editing, formatting, and verification of reported statistics. After
using this tool, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take
full responsibility for the content of the publication."

This statement is consistent with the APA Journals generative-AI policy and with
institutional guidance on the responsible use of generative AI in research.
